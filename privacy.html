DealerCue

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

What DealerCue uses

DealerCue uses your phone's camera and microphone to record walkaround videos and scan VINs, and saves finished videos to your Photos library. Videos stay on your phone until you choose to share them.

Vehicle lookups

When you scan or enter a VIN, it is sent to the U.S. government's NHTSA vehicle database to look up the car's specs. If your dealership connects an inventory feed, DealerCue reads it to find cars by stock number.

Talking points and fun facts

Talking points are built on your phone from the car's window sticker, brochure, your dealership's website and DealerCue's built-in model library. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

Your store's access

Your store unlocks DealerCue with a private store code. The app checks that code against a scrambled list on dealercue.app that doesn't reveal which stores use DealerCue.

Customer names

If you add a customer's first name to a video, it stays on your phone only and is cleared after 30 days. DealerCue has no accounts and no servers that store customer information.

What we don't do

DealerCue doesn't sell your data, doesn't show ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
