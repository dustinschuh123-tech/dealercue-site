Getting started

Download DealerCue, add your dealership name in Settings, then scan a VIN on the windshield or door jamb. DealerCue builds talking points for that car. Tap Start filming and the points scroll at the top of the screen while you film with the back camera.

Looking up cars by stock number

Once your store is set up, type a stock number and DealerCue pulls the car from your store's website. You can always scan or type the VIN too.

Talking points or full script

By default the teleprompter shows short talking points to riff off. Switch to a full script in Settings or from the controls on the filming screen.

Sending your video

When you finish, DealerCue writes a message for the customer you can edit. Pick how you send in Settings (your dealership's texting tool or iMessage). If a video is too big, tap Make it fit and it shrinks to the right size, still 1080p.
